Who we are

A practical agribusiness built for commercial value.

Patrick Stone Ventures Limited is an agribusiness-focused company with interests in cassava processing, agricultural commodity trading and greenhouse solutions.

The business is an early mover in Nigeria’s cassava peel processing industry. From its first factory in Iseyin, it processes and supplies cassava peel flour and cassava peel grits to small and large feed mills in Nigeria and the West African sub-region.

Our greenhouse construction and installation activities operate through Greenhouse.ng, while our commodity activity connects feed ingredients and agricultural products with commercial buyers.

About our company.

View all FAQs →

Patrick Stone Ventures Limited is a Nigerian agribusiness company involved in cassava processing, agricultural commodity sourcing and supply, and protected-agriculture infrastructure. It creates commercial value by processing cassava by-products, supplying agricultural commodities and providing greenhouse solutions through Greenhouse.ng.

Patrick Stone Ventures operates across three main areas: cassava processing, particularly cassava peel flour and grits; agricultural commodity sourcing and supply; and greenhouse design, construction, installation, irrigation and agronomy services through Greenhouse.ng.

Patrick Stone Ventures established its first cassava peel processing operation in Iseyin, Nigeria, in 2018. The business was developed around converting cassava-processing by-products into commercially useful agricultural products.

Patrick Stone Ventures primarily works with business and institutional buyers, including feed mills, animal-feed manufacturers, food processors, agricultural distributors, commercial farms, industrial processors, commodity buyers and agricultural investors.
