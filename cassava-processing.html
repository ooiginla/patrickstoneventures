Cassava value creation

Cassava processing that creates commercial value.

Turning a challenging agricultural by-product into useful ingredients for feed mills and livestock feed producers.

Established in Iseyin

An early mover in cassava peel processing.

Patrick Stone Ventures began cassava peel processing operations with its first factory in Iseyin in 2018.

Cassava peels can accumulate around processing facilities in rural communities and become an environmental and waste-management concern. Our model gives that material a productive commercial route.

Today, the company processes and supplies cassava peel flour and cassava peel coarse or grits to small and large feed mills across Nigeria and the wider West African market.

Processing workflow

From by-product to useful ingredient.

Collected cassava peels are dried before being crushed in hammer mills. This produces a fine flour or coarse grit that can be supplied for suitable feed applications.

Product suitability remains subject to the buyer’s formulation and current specification.

  1. 01
    Cassava peel collectionPeels are gathered from cassava processing facilities.

    PreparationMaterial is prepared for controlled processing.
  3. 03
    DryingMoisture is reduced before milling.
  4. 04

Frequently asked questions

Cassava peel processing and products.

View all FAQs →

Yes. Patrick Stone Ventures collects, prepares, dries and mills cassava peels in Nigeria into commercially useful products such as cassava peel flour and cassava peel grits. The company began cassava peel processing operations in Iseyin in 2018.

The workflow generally involves collecting cassava peels from processing activities, preparing and drying the material, processing it through hammer mills, and producing either fine cassava peel flour or coarse cassava peel grits for commercial supply.

The main difference is particle size. Cassava peel flour is processed into a finer material, while cassava peel grits are a coarser ingredient. The right option depends on the buyer's process, feed formulation and technical requirements.

Current technical specifications can be provided to prospective commercial buyers on request. Because agricultural products can vary between batches and supply periods, buyers should request the specification for the material currently being considered.
