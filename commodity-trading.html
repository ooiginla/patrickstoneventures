Sourcing and supply

Reliable agricultural commodity supply.

Connecting feed mills, processors, distributors and commercial buyers with agricultural commodities.

Commodity trading

Commercial supply built around your requirement.

Patrick Stone Ventures trades agricultural commodities that support animal feed, food and wider agricultural value chains.

Our portfolio includes soybean cake, palm kernel cake, maize, cassava flour and cassava starch. Brewery waste or brewers dried grain may also be considered where appropriate and available.

Every enquiry is handled against current availability, location, quantity and the buyer’s intended use. Technical specifications are supplied on request rather than assumed.
