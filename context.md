A few design decisions in that prompt are deliberate. The current site has only a very thin navigation structure and essentially presents the three activities as sections rather than giving the cassava products the depth they deserve. patrickstoneventures.com.ng Greenhouse.ng, however, already has considerably richer positioning around greenhouse kits, installation, irrigation/accessories, agronomy support and consultation, so the redesigned parent site should present that business clearly without duplicating the entire Greenhouse.ng site. Greenhouse.ng

I also deliberately made Products a major part of the redesign. For a business like this, someone arriving from Google searching for cassava peel flour supplier Nigeria, palm kernel cake supplier, cassava starch supplier, etc., should land on a proper product page rather than a generic homepage paragraph.

And I would not make it an online shop. The appropriate conversion flow here is:
Product → Product Information → Request Bulk Quote → WhatsApp / Email / Sales enquiry
That will make Patrick Stone Ventures look much more like a serious B2B agribusiness supplier.

One final recommendation: Codex to make the homepage hero exceptionally good. Something along the visual direction of large agricultural photography + deep forest green + cream + oversized typography + restrained gold/earth accents will transform the brand far more than adding lots of complicated functionality. The current company story—cassava processing since 2018, commodity trading and greenhouse solutions—is already enough to build a strong corporate site around.
