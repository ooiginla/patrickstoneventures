About Patrick Stone Ventures

Patrick Stone Ventures Limited is a Nigerian agribusiness company involved in cassava processing, agricultural commodity sourcing and supply, and protected-agriculture infrastructure. It creates commercial value by processing cassava by-products, supplying agricultural commodities and providing greenhouse solutions through Greenhouse.ng.

Patrick Stone Ventures Limited is headquartered at 7 Ibiyinka Olorunbe Street, Victoria Island, Lagos, Nigeria. The company serves agricultural businesses, processors, feed manufacturers, distributors, commercial farms and other buyers in Nigeria, while also considering regional supply enquiries.

Patrick Stone Ventures operates across three main areas: cassava processing, particularly cassava peel flour and grits; agricultural commodity sourcing and supply; and greenhouse design, construction, installation, irrigation and agronomy services through Greenhouse.ng.

Patrick Stone Ventures established its first cassava peel processing operation in Iseyin, Nigeria, in 2018. The business was developed around converting cassava-processing by-products into commercially useful agricultural products.

Patrick Stone Ventures primarily works with business and institutional buyers, including feed mills, animal-feed manufacturers, food processors, agricultural distributors, commercial farms, industrial processors, commodity buyers and agricultural investors.

FAQ topic

Cassava Processing

Yes. Patrick Stone Ventures collects, prepares, dries and mills cassava peels in Nigeria into commercially useful products such as cassava peel flour and cassava peel grits. The company began cassava peel processing operations in Iseyin in 2018.

Cassava peel flour is a processed agricultural ingredient made by drying cassava peels and milling them into a fine material. Patrick Stone Ventures supplies it primarily for suitable animal-feed applications, subject to the buyer's formulation requirements and the current product specification.

Cassava peel grits, sometimes called cassava peel coarse, are a coarser processed form of dried cassava peels. Patrick Stone Ventures supplies them to feed businesses and other commercial buyers for suitable animal-feed applications.

The workflow generally involves collecting cassava peels from processing activities, preparing and drying the material, processing it through hammer mills, and producing either fine cassava peel flour or coarse cassava peel grits for commercial supply.

Cassava peel flour and grits can be used as ingredients in suitable poultry, fish and other livestock-feed formulations. Final suitability and inclusion rates should be determined by the feed manufacturer or nutrition specialist using the current product specification.

Cassava peel flour can be considered for suitable poultry-feed formulations. Its suitability and inclusion level depend on the current nutritional specification and the overall formulation, so commercial buyers should request the applicable specification before use.

Processed cassava peel flour may be considered for suitable fish-feed formulations. Feed producers should assess it against their nutritional requirements and obtain the current specification before deciding whether it is appropriate.

The main difference is particle size. Cassava peel flour is processed into a finer material, while cassava peel grits are a coarser ingredient. The right option depends on the buyer's process, feed formulation and technical requirements.

Commercial buyers can contact Patrick Stone Ventures for current cassava peel flour specifications, quantity availability, packaging options, pricing and delivery arrangements. The company supplies feed mills, livestock-feed producers and agricultural distributors.

Patrick Stone Ventures supplies cassava peel grits to commercial buyers in Nigeria and considers regional enquiries. Buyers should provide the required quantity, delivery location and intended application to receive current supply information.

Current technical specifications can be provided to prospective commercial buyers on request. Because agricultural products can vary between batches and supply periods, buyers should request the specification for the material currently being considered.

Sustainability and Circular Agriculture

Patrick Stone Ventures creates productive uses for cassava peels generated during cassava processing. The peels are collected, dried and processed into flour and grits that can participate in suitable animal-feed value chains.

Cassava peels can accumulate around processing locations when they lack a productive outlet. Processing creates an alternative commercial use for the by-product while connecting cassava processors with feed-sector demand.

Yes. The cassava peel operation follows a circular-agriculture approach by turning an agricultural by-product into useful products for suitable applications instead of treating the peels solely as waste.

Cassava peel collection, preparation and processing can create opportunities for local participation in agricultural value chains. The model combines productive resource use with commercial activity around the communities where processing takes place.
