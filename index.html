The cassava opportunity

Creating value from cassava by-products.

When cassava peels accumulate around processing facilities, they can become a waste-management and environmental challenge for rural communities.

We create another route: the peels are dried and processed through hammer mills into flour or coarse grits that can serve feed-value-chain applications, subject to the buyer’s formulation and specification.

See our process
  1. 01
    Cassava processingThe value chain begins with cassava tubers.
  2. 02
    Peel collectionPeels are gathered for productive use.
  3. 03
    DryingMoisture is reduced before further processing.
  4. 04
    Hammer milling / grit productionProcessed into fine flour or coarse grits.
  5. 05
    Packaging & supplyPrepared for feed producers.

Responsible value creation

Turning agricultural waste into shared opportunity.

By creating productive applications for cassava peels, we support a more resource-efficient agricultural ecosystem. The model connects three forms of value: a practical response to agricultural waste, useful ingredients for feed producers, and work opportunities within the communities where processing takes place.
