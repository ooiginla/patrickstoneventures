Frequently asked questions

Cassava Peel Flour supply.

View all FAQs →

Cassava peel flour is a processed agricultural ingredient made by drying cassava peels and milling them into a fine material. Patrick Stone Ventures supplies it primarily for suitable animal-feed applications, subject to the buyer's formulation requirements and the current product specification.

Cassava peel flour can be considered for suitable poultry-feed formulations. Its suitability and inclusion level depend on the current nutritional specification and the overall formulation, so commercial buyers should request the applicable specification before use.

Processed cassava peel flour may be considered for suitable fish-feed formulations. Feed producers should assess it against their nutritional requirements and obtain the current specification before deciding whether it is appropriate.

Commercial buyers can contact Patrick Stone Ventures for current cassava peel flour specifications, quantity availability, packaging options, pricing and delivery arrangements. The company supplies feed mills, livestock-feed producers and agricultural distributors.
