Frequently asked questions

Cassava Peel Grits supply.

View all FAQs →

Cassava peel grits, sometimes called cassava peel coarse, are a coarser processed form of dried cassava peels. Patrick Stone Ventures supplies them to feed businesses and other commercial buyers for suitable animal-feed applications.

The main difference is particle size. Cassava peel flour is processed into a finer material, while cassava peel grits are a coarser ingredient. The right option depends on the buyer's process, feed formulation and technical requirements.

Patrick Stone Ventures supplies cassava peel grits to commercial buyers in Nigeria and considers regional enquiries. Buyers should provide the required quantity, delivery location and intended application to receive current supply information.

Current technical specifications can be provided to prospective commercial buyers on request. Because agricultural products can vary between batches and supply periods, buyers should request the specification for the material currently being considered.
